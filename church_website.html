About Us

Rooted in Faith,
Growing in Community

Since 1987, Grace Covenant has been a beacon of hope, healing, and transformation. We believe the Church is not a building — it is a people called to love God and love others.

✝️
Christ-Centered
Jesus is the foundation of everything we do and teach.
🤝
Community First
No one walks their faith journey alone. We carry each other.
🌍
Kingdom Impact
Our mission extends beyond the walls of the church.
📖
Truth-Grounded
Scripture is our authority and compass in all matters.

Our History

Milestones of Grace

1987
Founded
Grace Covenant established with 12 founding families in a rented hall.
1995
First Building
Moved into our first permanent church building on Mombasa Road.
2003
Youth Ministry
Launched our award-winning youth programme impacting 300+ teens.
2010
Expansion
Opened three satellite campuses across the county.
2018
Online Ministry
Launched live streaming — now reaching 40+ countries weekly.
2024
Today
2,400 members strong and growing in faith, love, and impact.
